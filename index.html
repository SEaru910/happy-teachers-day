To Ma’am Fatima,

When we look at the night sky, we see countless stars. Some are brighter, some are farther away, and some may seem small from where we stand. But somehow, every one of them has a light that reaches us.

When we think about you, Ma’am, we think of that kind of light

As our Values Education teacher, you have taught us things that go beyond lessons, activities, and written work. You have taught us to understand ourselves, be more aware of our emotions, treat others with respect, and think about the choices we make. And as our adviser, you have also been there for our section through so many little things—the reminders, the conversations, the moments when you have to call our attention, and even the times when you simply make sure that we are doing okay.

"I know our section is not always easy to handle. We can be loud, distracted, makulit, stubborn, and sometimes very very chaotic. There are probably days when we test your patience without even realizing it. But even with all of that, you still choose to guide us. You still remind us when we need it, correct us when we are wrong, and give us chances to do better."

And honestly, I think that is something we should be more grateful for.

Not every lesson you teach us will stay in our memory forever. We might forget certain activities, discussions, or things we once had to memorize. But I hope the values you have taught us stay. I hope we remember to be kinder, more understanding, more responsible, and more thoughtful toward the people around us.

Perhaps that is what your light means to us.

Like the light of a star traveling through the universe, the things you teach us may continue reaching us even long after we leave your classroom. Someday, when we are older and facing situations we cannot yet imagine, we might remember something you told us. Maybe a simple reminder from our Values Education class, something you said as our adviser, or even a lesson we did not think much about at the time.

And maybe that is when we will realize just how much you have taught us.

You are not only someone who teaches our section. You are someone who has been part of our everyday school life, someone who watches us grow, makes sure we stay on track, and helps us become better little by little.

So, Ma’am, from our whole section, thank you.

Thank you for your patience, your understanding, your effort, and for choosing to guide us even when we make it difficult. Thank you for being our Values Education teacher, but also for being our adviser—the person who gets to witness all our chaos, growth, mistakes, and little achievements along the way.

Happy Teacher's Day, Ma’am Fatima !

We may only be one small section in the enormous universe, but we are grateful that, for this part of our journey, we get to have you as one of the lights guiding us.

And wherever we go from here, we hope a little bit of the light you gave us goes with us. (◍•ᴗ•◍)

— With respect and gratitude, Section 8-Katmon
